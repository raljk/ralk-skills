## Discovery Proposal Template

### Standard Proposal Structure

A well-structured discovery proposal should include these sections:

```
1. Understanding the Opportunity
   ├── Background
   ├── The Ask
   └── The Problem

2. The Approach
   ├── Overall Approach
   ├── Deliverable 1: [Vision/Strategy]
   ├── Deliverable 2: [Experience Design]
   └── Considerations

3. Team & Timelines
   ├── How We'll Work Together
   ├── Timeline Options
   └── The Core Team

4. Commercials
   └── Investment Summary

5. Appendix
   ├── Design Method Cards
   ├── Previous Work Examples
   └── About Us
```

## Understanding the Opportunity

### Background Section Template

```markdown
## Background

[Client] has gone through significant transformation in the last [timeframe] 
with a shift towards [change description].

Due to these changes, there is [gap/challenge description].

The [team/department] has recently been stood up to address these gaps. 
However, due to [constraints], there is [current state problem].
```

**Illustrative example**: Use a fictional scenario or an approved public example tailored to the engagement.

### Discovery Deliverable Example: Future Vision

**Purpose**: Create alignment on a future experience vision and produce quality assets that can help sell the dream to start bringing the organisation on the journey.

#### Phase 1.1: Understand Context

**Key Activities**:
- Understand current experience, pains, gaps and opportunity areas
- Market and future trends scan
- Understand current product offerings
- Review existing research

**Outputs**:
- Current state experience maps across channels, products and key customer segments
- Competitor analysis report
- Summary of current customer pain points, experience gaps and opportunity areas

**Outcomes**:
- Clear view of what client's current gaps and opportunities are
- Understanding of what customers need and expect
- Clarity around market landscape and future trends

---

#### Phase 1.2: Establish & Align

**Key Activities**:
- Co-design vision statement and best practice experience principles
- Map high level future state experience
- Alignment on key enablers and dependencies
- Prioritisation of opportunity areas across channels and products
- High level viability assessment

**Outputs**:
- Vision statement
- Key experience principles
- High level experience map/storyboard of ideal state

**Outcomes**:
- Alignment on a vision and core CX principles across business units, channels and products
- A high level view of what the future state could look like
- High level understanding of the building blocks required to realise future vision

---

#### Phase 1.3: Ideate & Validate

**Key Activities**:
- Co-design of future state concepts
- Evaluate product elements and adoption
- Validation and iteration of concepts with customers
- Break down key enablers of the future experience
- Develop and finalise vision video
- DVF assessment

**Outputs**:
- Video of vision and hi-fidelity concept
- Finalised future vision experience map
- Finalised and validated conceptual designs
- DVF assessment
- Horizon roadmap
- Insights report including all research findings

**Outcomes**:
- Certainty that the future vision and experience is desirable, feasible and viable
- Further alignment and buy-in from the wider business on the 'north star' experience and vision
- Clear direction on what's achievable in the near-term vs. mid-long term

---

### Experience Design Deliverable Example

**Purpose**: Using the identified future vision, create a new E2E experience for [specific journey] that identifies foundational components needed to continue building towards the future vision.

#### Phase 2.1: Identify the Right Problem

**Key Activities**:
- Incorporate findings and insights from Deliverable 1
- Overlay future experience vision onto existing E2E journey and identify gaps
- Build out new E2E customer journeys and use-cases
- Identify required enablers and dependencies

---

#### Phase 2.2: Evaluate the Best Way Forward

**Key Activities**:
- Wireframing and mid-fidelity designs and prototyping
- Usability testing, validation and synthesis
- Design iteration based on user research
- Technical feasibility and viability assessments

---

#### Phase 2.3: Deliver Meaningful Outcomes

**Key Activities**:
- High fidelity designs for development
- Usability testing insights report and recommendations
- Establish foundational features to continue delivering towards the future vision
- Work alongside client to ensure smooth handover for delivery

**Outputs**:
- Low-Fidelity wires
- E2E flows and journey maps
- Usability testing insights report and recommendations
- Hi-fidelity, dev-ready designs validated through user research

## North Star / Vision Engagement

### Key Ingredients for Future Vision

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                 │
│   Market / cross-industry    ┐                                  │
│   trends, innovation,        │                                  │
│   insights                   │                                  │
│                              │     ┌──────────────────────┐     │
│   Current customer           ├────►│                      │     │
│   expectations / needs /     │     │   Future Experience  │     │
│   concerns                   │     │       Vision!        │     │
│                              │     │                      │     │
│   CX best practice          │     └──────────────────────┘     │
│   principles                 ┘                                  │
│                                                                 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

A future experience vision ensures that we craft a message and experience that is:
- **Relevant today** — meets current customer expectations
- **Relevant tomorrow** — surprises and delights with things they didn't expect
- **Best practice** — underpinned by CX principles

---

### Vision Engagement Outputs

| Output Type | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| North star vision | Inspiring video, sleek designs, interactive prototype |
| Experience principles | Design principles that guide future decisions |
| Horizon roadmap | Phased approach to realising the vision |
| Validated concepts | DVF-assessed designs ready for delivery planning |

### Building Foundations Pattern

When delivering a vision engagement, frame the foundational work:

```
                    Phase 1           Phase 2           Phase 3           Phase 4
                    
                 ┌──────────┐      ┌──────────┐      ┌──────────┐      ┌──────────┐
  North Star ───►│  [First  │─────►│  [Next   │─────►│  [Next   │─────►│  [Future │
                 │ Priority]│      │ Priority]│      │ Priority]│      │  State]  │
                 └──────────┘      └──────────┘      └──────────┘      └──────────┘
                       │                │                 │                 │
                       ▼                ▼                 ▼                 ▼
                  Leverage &       Leverage &        Leverage &       Leverage &
                   Uplift           Uplift            Uplift           Uplift
```

The foundations delivered for the first priority experience will help contribute to a framework for continued delivery across other channels, products and experiences.

## Strategy by Design Engagement

### When to Use

Use Strategy by Design when the client:
- Has products/services not delivering expected value
- Cannot decide which products/services to invest in
- Has high-level strategy not linked to practical roadmap
- Needs commercial certainty for large investments

### What You Deliver

| Deliverable | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| Business value roadmap | Not feature-based, focused on outcomes |
| Prioritisation method | Defined and usable method for prioritising |
| Strategy articulation | Practical, actionable articulation |
| Commercial certainty | Clear investment rationale |

---

### Strategy Workshop Structure (5 Days)

| Day | Focus | Activities |
|-----|-------|------------|
| 1 | Current State | Context Canvas, Business Model Canvas |
| 2 | Customer & Market | Journey Mapping, Value Proposition Design |
| 3 | Future State | Blue Ocean Strategy Canvas, Risks & Assumptions |
| 4 | Prioritisation | Lean Prioritisation, Success Metrics |
| 5 | Roadmap | Value-Based Roadmapping, Next Steps |

---

### Strategy Activities Menu

| Activity | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Value Proposition | Understand why someone should do business with you |
| Journey Mapping | Map end-to-end customer experience |
| Blue Ocean Strategy | Find unique value proposition to outlast competitors |
| Business Model Canvas | Explore value proposition, infrastructure, customers, finances |
| Risks & Assumptions | Identify and design tests for key assumptions |
| Context Mapping | Align on trends, competitors, uncertainties |
| Lean Prioritisation | Prioritise next steps using Lean methodology |
| Value-Based Roadmapping | Create milestones based on business value, not features |

## Assumptions Section Template

Include assumptions in every proposal:

```markdown
## Assumptions

• [Priority/scope assumption] is the first priority once the vision 
  and future experience has been defined

• In order to deliver by [deadline], there will be overlaps between 
  [phase A] and [phase B]

• There will be ongoing involvement of key decision makers and 
  stakeholders throughout the project

• A client designer (UX/UI) to be a part of the discovery process 
  (if possible)

• Access to data, previous research, insights and SMEs from [client]
```

## Considerations Section Template

Frame important strategic questions:

```markdown
## Things We'll Consider

While these are not deliverables for the current project, they are 
core elements that need to be considered for successful adoption:

• How might we ensure components are built to maximise reuse across 
  multiple applications?

• How might we ensure all designs going forward conform to our design 
  best practice principles?

• How might we federate out the building of a shared component library 
  through different teams?

• How might we better incorporate customer feedback into feature 
  enhancement and prioritisation?

• How might we increase the quality and consistency of customer 
  experiences without dramatically increasing economies of scale?
```
