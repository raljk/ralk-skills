# Case Study Template

Client-specific case studies are not included in this public edition. Do not invent past experience or imply that the examples below describe completed work.

## Add an approved case study

Use only material you have permission to share, or cite a verified public source.

- **Industry and context**: Describe the setting without unnecessary identifying details.
- **Challenge**: What problem needed to be understood or solved?
- **Approach**: Which research, synthesis, ideation, or validation methods were used, and why?
- **Outcome**: What changed? Include only supported results.
- **Limits and lessons**: What remains unknown, and what would you do differently?
- **Source and permission**: Record a public source or the basis for sharing.

## Finding relevant examples

Search by industry, problem type, method, and desired outcome. If no approved local example exists, say so and look for a suitable published case study. Distinguish sourced facts from your interpretation.
